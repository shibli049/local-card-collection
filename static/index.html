Delete album or box?

This removes the folder and any nested albums from your library. Its files are kept in the collection’s .card-atlas-trash recovery folder and will not appear when you reload.

Move selected images

    Photos move out of their current albums or boxes. Names and details stay with them. Paged albums use their first free cells; boxes have no position labels. Matching filenames or insufficient space will stop the move.

    Album layout

    Set the full album size, including empty pages, rows and cells. Existing photo positions stay in place. Photos without positions can be moved into empty cells.

    Drop onto a photo to swap positions. Filenames stay unchanged; arrangements are stored in this collection's catalog.

    Export albums as CSV

    Choose albums and boxes to export. Includes every card in the selected folders, regardless of the current search or result page.

    Administrator sign in

    Sign in to import photos, reload folders, and edit descriptions.

    Connect an existing folder

    Enter the full path to a folder on the computer running Card Atlas. Subfolders are included. Images stay in place. The catalog is stored as catalog.json in this folder.

    Names ending in -page-row-column (such as sample-12-3-2.jpg) get position labels automatically. Filenames without position numbers also work.
    GIVE YOUR PHOTOS A HOME

    Import photos

    Choose a new or existing album. Review conflicts before importing. Source photos stay in place.

    For a new collection, filenames without page-row-column numbers select Box automatically. Numbered filenames select Paged album. You can choose a different type before importing.

    Existing album: keep its row and column counts. Increase Pages to add room, or choose where this import starts.

    Drag photos here, or choose files

    JPG, PNG, GIF, WebP, BMP, AVIF or ICO. Up to 50 MiB per photo and 1 GiB per import.

    No photos selected

    Drop more photos to add to your selection. Choose individual files rather than folders.

    Fill columns from left to right, then the next row, then the next page. Positions start at 1.

    Select photos to preview their positions.

    Resolve conflicts

    Replace updates the existing photo and keeps its description. Rename keeps both: boxes use a numbered filename; paged albums use a free position. You can change each choice below.
